# 100-Article Editorial Roadmap

This roadmap is represented and validated in `src/content/article-roadmap.ts`. It contains exactly 100 original briefs: 25 Education/NCERT/CBSE, 20 Competitive Exams, 20 Technology/Internet, 20 How-to/Digital Utilities, and 15 India-focused guides.

Every record defines a search intent, heading plan, example, related tools, internal-link target, freshness classification, and official-source requirements. These are planning records, not published articles. Article routes remain unavailable until an editor has researched, written, fact-checked, sourced, and marked each piece as published.

## Editorial rules

1. Verify syllabus, dates, eligibility, notifications, policy, and official service instructions against the responsible official source immediately before publication.
2. Add a visible reviewed or verified date where freshness matters.
3. Demonstrate tasks with original examples; never copy or lightly rewrite another publisher.
4. Link only to functioning tools and reviewed articles.
5. Publish in batches after human review rather than generating all article bodies at once.

The TypeScript registry is the canonical itemised roadmap because automated tests enforce its count, distribution, uniqueness, completeness, relationships, and non-public status.
